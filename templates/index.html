❤️ cardiovascular Health Risk Assessment

A preventive tool to estimate your 10-year risk of heart disease using lifestyle and basic health indicators.

⚠️ This tool provides a statistical risk estimate and is not a medical diagnosis.

🫀 Why Heart Health Matters

Heart disease is one of the leading causes of death worldwide. Many cases can be prevented through early risk awareness and healthy lifestyle choices.

🥗 Lifestyle & Diet

A balanced diet, regular exercise, stress management, and avoiding smoking play a major role in keeping your heart healthy.

📊 Risk-Based Prediction

This system uses population-level data to estimate long-term cardiovascular risk rather than diagnosing current disease.

🧠 Early Awareness

Knowing your risk early helps you take preventive action before serious heart problems develop.
